Our Heritage

Where Every Bean Tells a Story

Founded on the belief that coffee is not just a drink, but a mindful ritual that unites people.

At Brew & Bean, we travel to sustainable coffee plantations across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Our small-batch roastery unlocks nuanced floral notes, delicate caramel undertones, and velvety chocolate textures in every extraction.

Micro-Roasting

Certified Baristas

Eco-Friendly

Cozy Atmosphere

Fast Wi-Fi, peaceful jazz, and plush seating for reading or work.
